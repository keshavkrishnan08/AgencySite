3 carousels a day. 60 days. The honest floor.

A deliberately conservative projection of posting three advice carousels a day to Instagram, TikTok and Facebook from zero. Carousels get less reach than Reels, most posts barely move, and only the occasional one breaks out. Treat this as the low end, not a target.

Reality check: slideshows are the slow lane. If these numbers feel small, that's the point — a brand-new account grinds for a while before anything compounds, and short video would very likely beat this. The realistic win here is the first handful of paying customers plus an audience you keep.
